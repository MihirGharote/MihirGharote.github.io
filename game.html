How to play

  • One of the 16 doors hides a treasure chest.
  • You have 4 magic stones. Drag a stone into a socket between two doors (or tap the stone, then tap a socket).
  • The stone always tells the truth: the treasure is to its left or to its right. Then it goes dark for good.
  • Tap a door to open it. Find the treasure!
