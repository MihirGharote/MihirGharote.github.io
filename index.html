How to play

A treasure hides behind one of 32 doors: 4 floors with 8 doors each.

  • 2 red stones go between two floors. They show if the treasure is above or below.
  • 3 blue stones go between two doors. They show if the treasure is left or right.
  • Tap a stone, then tap a glowing socket. Lightning marks the doors that are still possible. Greyed doors are out.
  • You may open only one door, and only when a single door is left.
